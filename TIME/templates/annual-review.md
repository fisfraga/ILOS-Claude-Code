# Annual Review Template

**Duration:** 3+ hours (spread across 2-3 sessions recommended)
**When:** Last week of December or first week of January
**Purpose:** The deepest review. Full life audit across all 12 Key Areas. Identity evolution. Next year visioning.

---

## Preparation

Before starting:
- Block 3+ hours across 2-3 sessions (do not rush this)
- Gather all quarterly reviews from this year
- Have your vision documents open (FPs, MTPs, HHGs, Life Manifesto)
- Find a quiet, comfortable space -- this is sacred time
