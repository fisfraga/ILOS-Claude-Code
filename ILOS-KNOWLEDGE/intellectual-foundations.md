# Intellectual Foundations of Intentional Life OS

The ILOS system is built on wisdom from 7+ years of study across personal growth, psychology, AI, habit design, knowledge management, philosophy, and spirituality. It is not just a productivity template -- it is a distillation of hundreds of books and frameworks, transformed into a practical system for intentional living.

---

## The Deeper Purpose

ILOS exists because **AI is as useful as the context it has about you.**

Generic AI gives generic advice. But when AI knows your vision, projects, wins, patterns, and specific life context, it becomes contextually enhanced intelligence -- a true thinking partner.

The deeper purpose is not productivity alone. It's about cultivating:
- **Presence** -- being fully engaged with what matters
- **Intention** -- consciously choosing your direction
- **Purpose** -- connecting daily actions to what truly matters

When more people live from intention, presence, and alignment, everything shifts.

## Key Teachers Who Shaped ILOS

### Benjamin Hardy

Shaped the ILOS worldview more than any other author. Key contributions:
- **Gap vs. Gain** -- Measure progress from where you started, not against an ideal. This mindset is woven throughout every review in ILOS.
- **10X Goals** -- Think bigger, not smaller. "SMART" goals are a disservice to ambition.
- **Future Self** -- Connecting to your future self as a source of motivation and clarity.

### Steven Kotler

Taught the power of purpose and ambitious goals:
- **Massively Transformative Purpose (MTP)** -- A guiding purpose big enough to inspire for decades.
- **High Hard Goals (HHG)** -- Multi-year objectives that completely change your life and spark massive motivation.
- **Flow** -- The state of optimal performance that emerges when challenge meets skill with clear goals.

### Tiago Forte

Introduced knowledge management as life infrastructure:
- **Building a Second Brain** -- Organizing knowledge to serve action.
- **PARA Method** -- Projects, Areas, Resources, Archive (adapted into ILOS's Life Overview with Areas, Projects, Topics).
- **Favorite Problems** -- Inspired by Richard Feynman. The ~12 questions that fascinate you and filter everything you encounter.

### Mindvalley (Life Book)

Inspired the holistic life mapping:
- **12 Key Life Areas** -- A meaningful life spans multiple dimensions: health, relationships, career, spirituality, creativity, finances, and more.
- **Define Vision, Purpose, and Strategy for each area** -- This became the VPS Framework in ILOS.
- The 12 areas connect to the 12 zodiac signs/houses as archetypal energies governing different life domains.

### Dragon Dreaming

A project design methodology combining ancient indigenous wisdom with modern systems thinking:
- **Four phases: Dream, Plan, Do, Celebrate** -- Directly infused into the VPER cycle (Vision, Plan, Execute, Review).
- **The missing stages:** Modern society excels at Plan and Do but skips Dreaming and Celebrating. These are the most important stages for alignment.
- Alignment is subjective and intuitive, accessible during quiet reflection, not busy execution.
