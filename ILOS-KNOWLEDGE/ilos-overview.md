# Intentional Life OS — System Overview

The Intentional Life OS (ILOS) is a complete life operating system built on Tana. It combines vision-aligned planning, AI coaching, and holistic life mapping into a single system for designing and living your life with intention.

---

## What It Is

ILOS is not a productivity system. It is a consciousness architecture -- a framework for aligning your daily actions with your deepest purposes, organized through cycles of time and enriched by AI partnership.

**The three things that make ILOS different:**
1. **Vision-connected planning** — Every weekly goal traces back to your life purpose
2. **AI coaching (PARACELSUS)** — An AI agent with full context on your life, not generic advice
3. **Holistic life mapping** — 12 areas of life, not just work

---

## What Makes ILOS Different

### Tana as a Structured Data Graph
Unlike flat-file or document-based systems, ILOS is built on Tana — a tool where every piece of information is a structured node in a graph. Goals, wins, projects, areas, and tasks are not just text; they are typed, tagged, and connected. This means your AI agent can query your life data precisely: "find all wins from this month connected to my Health area" or "show me active projects linked to this HHG."

Tana also provides native calendar integration — your plans, reviews, and daily nodes exist within a calendar structure, making time-based queries natural and powerful.

### Multi-Interface Access
Your life data is accessible through multiple interfaces, each suited to different modes of work:

- **Tana Native** — Visual outliner with dashboards, saved searches, and graph views. Best for browsing, organizing, and visual overview of your life system.
- **Claude Code (Conversational AI)** — Natural language interaction with PARACELSUS. Best for coaching conversations, reviews, planning sessions, and building.

The same data, two complementary ways to access it. Use Tana when you want to see and organize. Use Claude Code when you want to think, plan, review, or build.

### Bidirectional AI Partnership
PARACELSUS does not just read your data — it writes back. After a review session, wins are captured in Tana. After planning, goals are created. After building, tasks are checked off. This bidirectional flow means the AI is a true partner in your system, not just a consumer of your notes.

### Building & Creation
Claude Code agents can build real software, websites, dashboards, and integrations. Instructions and project context live in Tana (as projects, tasks, strategies). The agent reads from Tana what to build, then creates files, writes code, and runs commands in your local repository. Your knowledge informs your creation — and both live in the same system, accessed by the same agent.

## Core Principles

### 1. The VPER Cycle
Life flows through four repeating phases: Vision (Fire), Plan (Air), Execute (Earth), Review (Water). This cycle repeats fractally at every time scale — day, week, month, quarter, year. A fifth element, Alignment (Ether), weaves through all four, ensuring coherence.

### 2. Gap to Gain
Always measure progress against your past self, never against an ideal. Start every review with wins. There is no failure, only data and progress.

### 3. Extreme Prioritization
Maximum 3 goals per period. If you have more than 3 priorities, you have none. Focus ruthlessly on the vital few.

### 4. Review Before Plan
Before planning any new period, review the previous one. Reviewing first puts you in the Gain and grounds your planning in evidence of capability.

### 5. Intelligence Through Context
PARACELSUS knows YOUR vision, YOUR goals, YOUR wins, and YOUR personal context. This transforms AI from a generic tool into a true thinking partner.

## Practical Concepts

### The Purpose Cascade (North Star)

Every daily action traces back to your North Star:

```
Favorite Problems (curiosities that never stop)
  └→ MTPs (guiding purposes)
       └→ HHGs (multi-year goals)
            └→ Annual Goals → Quarterly → Monthly → Weekly → Daily Actions
```

### VPS Framework (Intentional Areas)

Each Key Area can have:
- **Vision** — ideal state for this area of life
- **Purpose** — why this area matters to you
- **Strategy Systems** — how to maintain and improve (repeated routines)

Intentional areas = contracts with yourself for thriving in each life domain.

### 10-15 Active Projects Rule

Fewer than 10 = stalling (always have something to switch to). More than 15 = scattered (no visible progress on any). Maintain weekly, refresh quarterly.

### Strategy Systems

Repeated actions at defined periodicity (daily/weekly/monthly) connected to specific Areas. Where strategies come from: Area standards + Goals = HOW you'll get there. Systems shape your life more than individual goals do.

### Super Folders

Areas, Projects, Topics serve as retrieval gateways. Every note, task, and meeting can be connected to its folder. Open a Project, see everything related. This solves the hardest PKM problem: actually finding what you captured.

### Voice-Powered Capture

Speaking activates more gratitude, is 3-4x faster than typing. Record reflections, and AI extracts wins and tasks. Removes friction and captures unconscious priorities.

### The 80/20 of Planning

You only need 2 tasks per cycle:
1. **Set 3 Big Goals** at the start
2. **List all your Wins** at the end

This is the core of the system. Everything else builds on this foundation.

## How It Works in Practice

1. **Set your vision** — Define Favorite Problems, MTPs, and HHGs (use `/north-star`)
2. **Map your life** — Organize into 12 areas, active projects, and topics of interest
3. **Plan in cycles** — Set 3 goals per period (year > quarter > month > week)
4. **Execute daily** — Take aligned action through tasks, systems, and routines
5. **Review and celebrate** — Capture wins, recognize patterns, adjust course
6. **Let PARACELSUS help** — AI coaching grounded in YOUR context, not generic advice

The week is the core rhythm. If you maintain nothing else, maintain the weekly review and plan.

## The Knowledge-Action Bridge

One of the most powerful aspects of ILOS is that knowledge and creation live in the same system, accessed by the same agent.

In traditional setups, your notes are in one app, your code is in another, your project management is in a third, and your AI tools have no context on any of them. ILOS eliminates this fragmentation:

- **Knowledge** (Areas, Topics) and **Creation** (Projects, Products) share the same SPACE dimension
- Your AI agent (PARACELSUS) can read your area strategies while building your product
- Project context in Tana (desired outcomes, tasks, related notes) directly informs what gets built in your repository
- Progress flows back to Tana — tasks checked off, wins captured, notes created

This means building apps, websites, dashboards, and code is not a separate activity from your life system — it is a natural extension of it. Your knowledge informs your creation. Your creation lives alongside your knowledge. Same folder, same agent, no disconnect.
