## The Intentional Planning Cycle

### 1. Cycles of Time

First of all, it's important to understand that **life happens in Cycles of Time**.

**Cycles of Time: From Year → Quarter → Month → Week → Day**

Second, remember that **the act of planning is more important than the plan**.

When you bring these together, this means that planning your year is a **continuous process**.

The yearly plan is not a blueprint of everything you'll achieve in the year.

Rather, the yearly plan is a **commitment**.

It's not only a commitment to stop, reflect, and plan the Year…

**MOST IMPORTANTLY, it's a commitment to stop, reflect, and plan the intermediary cycles of Quarters, Months and Weeks.**

Planning is an ongoing process.

You can't just plan the entire year in one go, and never look at it again…

P.S. This is exactly why people abandon their yearly plans… They are not committed to the process. They don't view planning as an ongoing process, but rather as a one-time thing.

**The secret to keep up with your plan is this:**

Swap the perfect plan for intentional cycles.

### 2. The Four Stages

The Intentional Planning Cycle is composed of 4 stages:

1. **Vision**
2. **Plan**
3. **Execute**
4. **Review**

Every Year, Quarter, Month and Week, I go through this cycle.

Begin the cycle with a **Vision**, a clear direction, then take time to reflect and **Plan** your priorities for the cycle. The most important thing is to **Execute** what needs to be done, and finally **Review** your progress.

It's worth noting that each periodicity has a different planning cycle.

And also super important to mention that **you don't need 100% success rate to benefit from this**… It's normal to skip a week, and sometimes even a month.

But here's the catch..!

Things get interesting when you connect everything.

### 3. The Holistic Second Brain Cycle

When you bring in elements of a Second Brain, such as:
- Areas
- Projects
- Tasks
- Notes

…into the Intentional Planning Cycle…

It becomes the **Holistic Second Brain Cycle**.

Now, your entire Second Brain is connected through the cycles of planning.

And each stage has a digital home inside your Second Brain.

## How to Start Planning Your Year

Before we talk about the intermediary cycles, let's talk about planning the year.

Even though planning is more important than the plan, you should still have a Plan for the year.

After all, you arrive at a plan through the process of planning.

Meanwhile, the process of planning revolves around **deep reflection**.

A solid reflection will encompass your **Past**, your **Present** and specially, a connection to your **Future Self**.

### The Four Steps

#### Step 1: Measure the Gain
**What progress have you made in the past?**

List all your wins for the past year: accomplishments, challenges you've overcome, important people you've met, lessons you've learned, milestones, ways in which you have evolved, etc.

This will put you into a state of confidence, motivation and courage to strive for big accomplishments.

#### Step 2: Visualize Your Future Self
**How can you 10X your life in the next 3 years?**

People underestimate what they can achieve in 3 years time. You can radically change your life in 3 years, but most people don't even try.

This is the moment you stop aiming for mediocre.

Write out (in details) exactly how your 3 years Future Self is 10X better than you today.

#### Step 3: Reflect on Your Priorities
**What are your priorities now?**

Given the Future Self you're picturing, what are your priorities right now?

Prioritize where you will focus your attention on for this year.

> "If you have more than 3 priorities, you have none"
> — Jim Collins

Pick the top 3 priorities to take you from today to the level of your Future Self.

What are the 3 most important Areas in your life?

#### Step 4: Set Big Goals
**What will you achieve this year?**

From the top 3 priorities, it's time to set goals for the year.

Remember: **Goals are supposed to give you a direction. Go Big.**

It's never a problem to fall short from a Goal. Life is too short to care about adding a checkmark inside a checkbox.

What matters is **progress**.

And Big Goals EXCITE you, inspire you, and motivate you towards progress.

Use them wisely.

## Don't Stop! Keep Planning

This is just the beginning of the journey.

After you kick-off the planning process for the year, it's time to keep going. Planning the year will keep on happening along the entire year.

Luckily, now you are familiar with the process.

**Repeat the Intentional Planning Cycle for the 4 Quarters, 12 Months and 52 Weeks of the year.**

This seems like a lot, but you only have to deal with 1 quarter, 1 month and 1 week at a time.

**Life Overview with Active Cycles**

Best of all: **The process is super simple.**

You only have 2 tasks for each cycle:

1. **Set 3 Big Goals**
2. **List all your Wins**

This is the 80/20 of planning and reviewing.

Set 3 Big Goals at the start of every quarter, month and week. Then look back and measure the gain for every week, month and quarter.

This way you are striving for big goals, but you're focused on **how much progress you're making** instead of how much is left until you reach your goal.

## Key Principles

### What Makes This Different

1. **Planning is a Process, Not a One-Time Event**
   - Continuous reflection across multiple time cycles
   - Commitment to the process, not just the outcome

2. **Progress Over Perfection**
   - Focus on how much you've gained
   - Not how much is left to achieve

3. **Big Goals + Healthy Detachment**
   - Set audacious goals for direction
   - Stay flexible and adaptable

4. **Cycles Connect Everything**
   - Year → Quarter → Month → Week → Day
   - Each cycle informs the next

5. **Integration with Your Second Brain**
   - Planning connects to Projects, Areas, Tasks
   - Creates a holistic system

6. **Simplicity at the Core**
   - Just 2 tasks per cycle: Set Goals + List Wins
   - 80/20 of planning effectiveness
