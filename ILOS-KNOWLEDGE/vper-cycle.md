# The VPER Cycle: Vision, Plan, Execute, Review

The VPER cycle is the core rhythm of Intentional Life Design. It describes how intentional living operates as a continuous loop of four phases, each mapped to an elemental archetype, repeating fractally at every time scale from a single day to an entire year.

---

## The Cycle

```
... --> VISION (Fire) --> PLAN (Air) --> EXECUTE (Earth) --> REVIEW (Water) --> ...
                              ^                                      |
                              |          ALIGNMENT (Ether)           |
                              +--------------------------------------+
```

The cycle never ends. Each Review feeds into the next reconnection with Vision, which informs the next Plan, which drives the next Execution. Alignment (Ether), the fifth element, weaves through all four phases, connecting the individual cycle to your larger purpose and ensuring coherence across every dimension of your life.

---

## The Four Phases

### Vision -- Fire

- **Purpose:** Ignite direction and purpose. Connect to what matters.
- **Energy:** Expansive, inspirational, future-oriented. Fire illuminates the path and concentrates vital energy into a clear intention.
- **Elemental Qualities:** Fire is desire, passion, and the spark of life. Without it, you are lost in darkness with no sense of where you want to go. A clear vision is a concentration of energy that creates the flame of motivation.
- **Practical Meaning:** Reconnect with your Massively Transformative Purposes, High Hard Goals, Favorite Problems, and the vision you hold for each area of your life. Ask: What inspires me for this period? What is the ideal I am drawn toward?

### Plan -- Air

- **Purpose:** Give direction to the fire. Channel energy into focused intention.
- **Energy:** Intellectual, strategic, directive. Air moves the fire in the direction you choose.
- **Elemental Qualities:** Air is the mind, the intellect, the power of thought. It gives direction to vision. Without air, fire spreads aimlessly. But too much air without grounding creates overthinking -- a vortex of plans that never reach action.
- **Practical Meaning:** Set a maximum of 3 Clear Goals for the period. Identify projects, strategy systems, and specific actions that serve those goals. Connect goals upward to your vision and downward to executable steps. Keep it simple: the act of planning matters more than the plan itself.

### Execute -- Earth

- **Purpose:** Ground intention into reality through consistent action.
- **Energy:** Stable, reliable, material. Earth is the fuel that keeps the fire burning.
- **Elemental Qualities:** Earth is matter, action, repetition, and stability. It is the most grounding element and the bottleneck for most people. Without earth, the cycle never completes -- vision remains a dream, plans remain ideas. Earth is what makes the fire catch and sustain.
- **Practical Meaning:** Work on your projects. Complete your tasks. Execute your strategy systems and routine actions. This is where transformation happens in the material world -- one action at a time, one day at a time.

### Review -- Water

- **Purpose:** Integrate experience into wisdom. Celebrate progress. Carry memory forward.
- **Energy:** Reflective, receptive, integrative. Water flows back to the source, carrying everything learned.
- **Elemental Qualities:** Water holds memory. Your body is 70% water. Yet review is the most neglected phase in modern life. Without intentional review, you store memories unconsciously -- often from a place of lack rather than abundance. Intentional review means choosing what memory you carry forward about yourself and your progress.
- **Practical Meaning:** List your wins. Celebrate what you accomplished. Recognize patterns. Extract lessons. Feel the progress before moving on.

### Alignment -- Ether (The Fifth Element)

- **Purpose:** Connect the four phases into coherent wholeness. Link each individual cycle to your universal purpose.
- **Energy:** Connective, holistic, unifying. Ether is not a separate phase but the thread that runs through all four.
- **Practical Meaning:** When executing, stay connected to your vision. When planning, draw on what you learned in your last review. When reviewing, let your reflections point toward what comes next. Alignment means no phase operates in isolation.

## The "Review Before Plan" Principle

The system could just as easily be called "Review and Plan," because that is the real order. Before planning any new period, review the previous one.

- **Why it matters:** Reviewing first puts you in the Gain -- you see how far you have come before deciding where to go next. This grounds your planning in evidence of your own capability, not in an abstract ideal.
- **The sequence:** Complete last period's Review, then begin this period's Plan. The review sets the tone, the emotional state, and the factual foundation for what comes next.
- **What happens when you skip it:** Planning without reviewing is reacting to what is loudest rather than responding to what matters. You lose the compounding benefit of pattern recognition across cycles.

---

## The Review Sequence

When conducting a review at any time scale, follow this order:

1. **Celebrate** -- Start with wins. What are you proud of? Let the progress land emotionally.
2. **Recognize** -- Point out patterns. What themes emerge across your wins? What worked consistently?
3. **Enhance** -- Look deeper at the material from this period. Are there wins you missed or undervalued?
4. **Reflect** -- What did this period teach you? What surprised you? How are you different?
5. **Redirect** -- What will you carry forward into the next period? What will you leave behind?
6. **Energize** -- What excites you about what comes next? End with energy and forward momentum.

## Fractal Repetition Across Time Scales

The VPER cycle is not something you do once. It repeats at every level of time, like a fractal pattern where each scale contains the same structure:

| Time Scale | Character | Duration | VPER Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Year** | The transformative arc | Annual cycle | Identity shifts, major direction. Who will you become? |
| **Quarter** | Strategic 13-week cycles | ~3 months | Major deliverables, strategic milestones. What will be different in 13 weeks? |
| **Month** | Essential monthly focus | ~4 weeks | Concrete projects and outcomes. What will you ship this month? |
| **Week** | The core rhythm | 7 days | Focused execution on the vital few. What 3 things make this week successful? |
| **Day** | Micro-intentions (optional) | 1 day | Single most important action. What is the ONE thing today? |

Each level nests within the one above. Weekly goals serve monthly objectives. Monthly goals serve quarterly milestones. Quarterly milestones serve the annual vision. The annual vision serves your Massively Transformative Purpose.

### Practical Application at Each Scale

**Year:**
- Vision: Reevaluate your MTP, High Hard Goals, and Favorite Problems. Set a strategic theme for the year.
- Plan: Define 3 major annual goals. Map initial focus for each quarter.
- Execute: Live the year through its nested quarterly, monthly, and weekly cycles.
- Review: Conduct a deep annual review (3+ hours). Celebrate 52 weeks of wins. Reflect on identity evolution.

**Quarter:**
- Vision: Reconnect with the annual vision and relevant High Hard Goals.
- Plan: Define 3 quarterly goals. Map focus to months. Update areas and strategies as needed.
- Execute: Drive progress through monthly and weekly cycles.
- Review: Review 13 weeks of wins (1-2 hours). Assess goal status. Update personal context.

**Month:**
- Vision: Review the quarterly goals for context and direction.
- Plan: Define 3 monthly goals. Identify area focus for the month.
- Execute: Work through weekly cycles with monthly goals as context.
- Review: Celebrate monthly wins (30-60 minutes). Notice which areas got attention and which were neglected.

**Week:**
- Vision: Glance at monthly and quarterly goals for alignment.
- Plan: Define 3 weekly goals. Check strategy systems due this week. Allocate days.
- Execute: Complete tasks, projects, routine actions. This is where the work happens.
- Review: List weekly wins (20-30 minutes). Assess goal completion. Capture insights.

**Day (optional):**
- Vision: See this week's goals for context.
- Plan: Set 1-3 daily intentions. Check strategies due today.
- Execute: Do the work.
- Review: Capture daily gratitude and quick wins (5 minutes).

## The Elemental Connection Summary

| Phase | Element | Quality | Manifests As |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vision | Fire | Illumination, desire, passion | Purpose, direction, motivation |
| Plan | Air | Intellect, direction, strategy | Goals, structure, clarity |
| Execute | Earth | Stability, matter, repetition | Actions, projects, systems |
| Review | Water | Memory, feeling, integration | Wins, reflection, celebration |
| Alignment | Ether | Connection, wholeness, coherence | Unity between all phases and your larger purpose |

The path to a well-designed life comes from developing strength in all four elements. If you are strong in fire and air but weak in earth, you dream and plan beautifully but never complete. If you are strong in earth but neglect water, you work tirelessly but never celebrate or learn from your progress. Balance across the elements is the alchemical work of intentional living.
