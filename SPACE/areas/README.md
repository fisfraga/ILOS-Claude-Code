# The 12 Key Areas

Your life is organized across 12 Key Areas -- ongoing responsibilities and domains that never truly complete. Unlike projects (which have endpoints), areas require continuous attention and intentional design throughout your life.

The 12 areas are inspired by the 12 astrological houses, representing fundamental aspects of human experience. You do not need to know or care about astrology to use them -- they simply provide a comprehensive map that covers all dimensions of a well-lived life.

---

## The 12 Areas

### 1. Health & Vitality
Physical health, body, energy, fitness, nutrition, sleep, and overall vitality. This is the foundation -- without health, nothing else works at full capacity.

### 2. Finances & Resources
Money, investments, possessions, financial security, and your relationship with material resources. Includes both earning and managing.

### 3. Intellectual Life
Learning, reading, writing, communication, curiosity, skill development, and mental growth. How you engage with ideas and information.

### 4. Home & Family
Family relationships, home environment, roots, emotional foundation, and domestic life. The private base from which everything else operates.

### 5. Creative Expression
Creativity, art, play, hobbies, self-expression, joy, and fun. The things you do for the pure pleasure of creating and expressing yourself.

### 6. Routines & Work Life
Daily routines, work habits, service, systems, and the practical mechanics of how you operate day-to-day. Where discipline meets daily life.

### 7. Love Relationship
Romantic partnership, marriage, and committed one-on-one relationships. How you show up in your most intimate connection.

### 8. Emotional Life
Emotional depth, transformation, healing, therapy, shared resources, and the inner work of processing life's intensity.

### 9. Life Vision & Meaning
Philosophy, higher purpose, travel, adventure, big-picture thinking, and the search for meaning. Where you connect to something larger than yourself.

### 10. Career & Public Life
Professional vocation, public reputation, leadership, ambition, and your contribution to the world through work.

### 11. Social Life & Community
Friendships, groups, community involvement, social causes, and your place in the broader network of people.

### 12. Spirituality & Inner Life
Meditation, contemplation, solitude, dreams, the unconscious, and your connection to the transcendent -- however you define it.

---

## The VPS Framework

Each area can have three intentional components:

- **Vision** -- What does "thriving" look like in this area? Paint the ideal picture.
- **Purpose** -- Why does this area matter to you? What is at stake?
- **Strategy** -- What recurring systems and routines maintain and improve this area?

When an area has all three defined, it becomes an **Intentional Area** -- a contract with yourself for how you will show up in this domain of life.

## How Areas Connect to the Rest of ILOS

- **Goals** (TIME) are set within the context of areas -- "Improve Health area by running 3x/week"
- **Projects** (SPACE) serve specific areas -- "Kitchen renovation project" serves Home & Family
- **Wins** (MEMORY) are tagged by area -- enables area-by-area progress tracking
- **Strategy Systems** (TIME) are assigned to areas -- "Daily meditation" serves Spirituality
- **Reviews** surface which areas received attention and which were neglected
