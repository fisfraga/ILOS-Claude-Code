# Personal Context

Your vision and goals tell PARACELSUS **what** you want. Personal context tells PARACELSUS **who** you are -- your rhythms, constraints, relationships, and the realities that shape every decision.

---

## Why Personal Context Matters

Without personal context, AI coaching is generic. With it, PARACELSUS becomes a true partner:

- **Without:** "You should exercise 5 times a week"
- **With:** "Given your energy dip after 3pm and your kids' school pickup at 3:30, morning exercise fits best. Your 6am window before the house wakes up is your superpower."

The more you share, the more personalized and useful PARACELSUS becomes.

---

## The 9 Categories

Each file covers a different dimension of who you are:

| # | Category | What It Covers |
|---|----------|---------------|
| 0 | **Life Vision & Manifesto** | Your deepest vision, beliefs, ideal life, who you're becoming |
| 1 | **Routines & Habits** | Your daily and weekly rhythm |
| 2 | **Energy Patterns** | Chronotype, optimal conditions, drains and sources |
| 3 | **Relationships** | Key people, social orientation, commitments |
| 4 | **Non-Negotiables** | Boundaries, constraints, things you protect |
| 5 | **Work Patterns** | Professional context, career stage, work style |
| 6 | **Geography & Lifestyle** | Location, living situation, practical constraints |
| 7 | **Life Stage** | Current season, transitions, bandwidth |
| 8 | **Identity & Values** | Who you are, what success means to you |

Category 0 connects directly to your Favorite Problems, MTPs, and HHGs in `QUANTUM/vision/` — it is the foundation that all other context rests upon.

---

## How to Fill These In

1. **Start with what feels easiest.** You don't need to complete everything at once.
2. **Write naturally.** These are for you and your AI coach, not for publication.
3. **Be honest.** The more accurate your context, the better the coaching.
4. **Iterate over time.** Fill in what you can now, add more as you discover things about yourself.
5. **Update during quarterly reviews.** Life changes — your context files should change with it.

---

## When PARACELSUS Uses This

PARACELSUS reads these files when:
- Planning your day or week (to respect your energy patterns and constraints)
- Suggesting goals (to ensure they're realistic for your situation)
- Providing coaching (to understand the full picture, not just the task)
- Doing alignment checks (to connect your actions to your deeper values)

You don't need to tell PARACELSUS about this information in conversation — it reads these files directly.

---

## Where to Start

Start with the files that feel most natural and concrete:

1. **Category 8 (Identity & Values)** — Write your core values and how you see yourself
2. **Category 7 (Life Stage)** — Describe your current season of life
3. **Category 1 (Routines & Habits)** — Document your actual daily rhythm

Then fill in the remaining categories (2-6) at your own pace. **Category 0 (Life Vision & Manifesto)** is the deepest one — it is okay if it takes time to emerge. Save it for when you have more clarity.

## Tana Sync (Optional)

If you use Tana with the ILOS integration, your personal context files can be mirrored as Tana nodes for visual browsing and graph connectivity.

- **Local files** — Read by PARACELSUS during Claude Code sessions (coaching, planning, alignment)
- **Tana nodes** — Browseable in Tana's visual interface, searchable, connectable to other nodes

Run `/tana-setup` to configure bidirectional sync, then `/sync` to keep local files and Tana in sync.

---

## Archive & Evolution

Personal context is not static — it evolves as you do. When **major changes** happen (identity shift, career change, move, relationship transition), snapshot the previous version before updating.

**Archive in Tana:** A "Personal Context Archive" node stores dated snapshots of what changed and when.

**Archive locally (optional):** You can keep previous versions in a `PERSONAL-CONTEXT/archive/` subfolder with dated filenames (e.g., `archive/identity-values-2026-Q1.md`).

This creates a record of your evolution over time — valuable for annual reviews and seeing how far you've come.

---

## Update Cadence

| Review Cycle | What Typically Updates | Examples |
|-------------|----------------------|----------|
| **Weekly** | Small energy/work pattern observations | "Nature walks are my best ideation time" |
| **Monthly** | Relationship dynamics, geographic changes, work rhythm shifts | "Started working with a new collaborator" |
| **Quarterly** | Identity, values, life stage, non-negotiables | "Transitioning from building phase to maintenance" |
| **Annually** | Life vision, manifesto, deep identity review | Full Category 0 refresh |

After reviews, the `/context-update` skill scans for personal context signals and proposes updates to these files.
