# High Hard Goals (HHGs)

## What Are HHGs?

The concept comes from **Steven Kotler** in *The Art of Impossible*. High Hard Goals are multi-year objectives that take thousands of hours to pursue successfully. They are the most powerful type of goal in terms of motivation because they are strongly aligned with your life purpose.

### Key characteristics:
- **High:** Ambitious enough to change your life completely when achieved
- **Hard:** Require significant effort, skill development, and persistence (measured in years)
- **Connected:** Each HHG serves at least one of your MTPs
- **Motivating:** They spark energy and excitement, not dread

HHGs bridge the gap between your Massively Transformative Purposes (abstract, lifelong) and your Annual Goals (concrete, time-bound). They typically span 2-5 years.

### Examples
- "Build and launch a SaaS product that reaches 1,000 paying customers" (serves MTP about democratizing access)
- "Write and publish a book on intentional living" (serves MTP about helping people design their lives)
- "Achieve financial independence through my own business" (serves MTP about freedom and creativity)

## The Purpose Cascade

```
Favorite Problems (curiosities that never stop)
  └→ MTPs (guiding purposes)
       └→ HHGs (multi-year goals — you are here)
            └→ Annual Goals → Quarterly → Monthly → Weekly → Daily Actions
```

## Healthy Detachment

Remember: be **heavily committed to the target but healthily detached from the outcome**. HHGs give you direction and motivation, but measuring your progress (the Gain) matters more than reaching the exact destination. If something better appears along the way, take it.
