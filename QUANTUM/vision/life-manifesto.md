# Life Manifesto

Your Life Manifesto is a living declaration of who you are becoming and what you stand for. It is not a fixed document -- it evolves as you do. The purpose is to anchor your daily decisions in something deeper than circumstance.

## My Ideal Life Vision

*Paint a vivid picture of the life you are designing. Write it as if it is already happening -- present tense, full of sensory detail. This is not fantasy; it is intentional design.*

**Guiding questions:**
- What does my ideal morning look like?
- Where am I living? With whom?
- What work am I doing, and how does it feel?
- How do I spend my free time?
- What does my body feel like? My mind?
- What relationships surround me?

## The Impact I Want to Make

*How do you want to contribute? What legacy are you building? This section connects your personal life to something larger than yourself.*

**Guiding questions:**
- Who do I want to help, and how?
- What would I create if I knew I could not fail?
- What problem in the world am I uniquely positioned to address?
- When I look back at my life in 30 years, what will I be most proud of?
