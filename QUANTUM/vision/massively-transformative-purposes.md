# Massively Transformative Purposes (MTPs)

## What Are MTPs?

The concept comes from **Steven Kotler** (and Peter Diamandis) in *The Art of Impossible*. A Massively Transformative Purpose is a guiding star -- a purpose so big that it could inspire you for years or decades. It is aimed at transformation beyond personal benefit.

MTPs are not goals you check off. They are **directions you orient your life toward**. They sit between your Favorite Problems (pure curiosity) and your High Hard Goals (concrete multi-year objectives) in the Purpose Cascade.

### Characteristics of a Good MTP
- **Massively:** It affects a large number of people or has wide impact
- **Transformative:** It fundamentally changes something about how people live, think, or operate
- **Purpose:** It gives your life direction and meaning beyond personal success

### Examples
- "Democratize access to self-knowledge tools so anyone can design their life with intention"
- "Make every child on Earth a lifelong learner"
- "Eliminate the gap between what science knows and what business does"

## The Purpose Cascade

```
Favorite Problems (curiosities that never stop)
  └→ MTPs (guiding purposes — you are here)
       └→ HHGs (multi-year goals)
            └→ Annual Goals → Quarterly → Monthly → Weekly → Daily Actions
```

Your MTPs bridge the gap between abstract curiosity and concrete action. Every High Hard Goal should serve at least one MTP.

---

*Review your MTPs annually. They should evolve slowly -- if they change dramatically every year, they may not be deep enough. If they never change, you may not be growing.*
