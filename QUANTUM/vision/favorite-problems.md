# Favorite Problems

## What Are Favorite Problems?

The concept comes from Nobel Prize-winning physicist **Richard Feynman**, who famously said:

> "You have to keep a dozen of your favorite problems constantly present in your mind, although by and large they will lay in a dormant state. Every time you hear or read a new trick or a new result, test it against each of your twelve problems to see whether it helps. Every once in a while there will be a hit, and people will say, 'How did he do it? He must be a genius!'"

Favorite Problems are not problems to solve -- they are **questions you cannot stop thinking about**. They are the curiosities that follow you across years, the themes that show up in your reading, your conversations, and your daydreams. They act as a filter for everything you encounter: when a new idea, book, or opportunity appears, your Favorite Problems help you instantly recognize whether it is relevant to your deeper interests.

In ILOS, Favorite Problems sit at the very top of the Purpose Cascade -- they are the seed from which your MTPs, HHGs, and eventually your daily actions grow.

## How to Discover Yours

- What topics make you lose track of time?
- What questions keep coming back to you over years?
- What would you study if you had unlimited time and money?
- What conversations light you up?
- What injustices or inefficiencies bother you most?

## Examples

1. *How can technology amplify human wisdom rather than replace human judgment?*
2. *What does it look like to build a business that makes you healthier, not sicker?*
3. *How do ancient wisdom traditions and modern science describe the same truths in different languages?*
