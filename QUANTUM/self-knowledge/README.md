# Self-Knowledge

Self-knowledge is the foundation of intentional living. The better you understand your nature -- your strengths, growth edges, decision-making style, and energetic patterns -- the more effectively you can design your life.

---

## Why Self-Knowledge Matters in ILOS

Generic productivity systems treat everyone the same. ILOS recognizes that you are unique -- your optimal routines, decision-making process, energy patterns, and growth path are specific to you. Self-knowledge tools help you discover and document these patterns so your AI coach can provide truly personalized guidance.

---

## Frameworks You Can Use

Any self-knowledge framework that resonates with you belongs here. Common ones include:

### Personality & Strengths
- **CliftonStrengths (StrengthsFinder)** -- Your top 5 talent themes
- **MBTI / 16 Personalities** -- Cognitive preferences and information processing style
- **Enneagram** -- Core motivation patterns and growth paths
- **Big Five (OCEAN)** -- Research-backed personality dimensions
- **DISC** -- Communication and behavioral style

### Values & Purpose
- **VIA Character Strengths** -- Your signature character strengths
- **Ikigai** -- Intersection of passion, mission, vocation, and profession
- **Personal Values Assessment** -- Your hierarchy of values

### Energy & Body
- **Chronotype** -- Your natural sleep/wake cycle (lion, bear, wolf, dolphin)
- **Ayurvedic Dosha** -- Constitutional body-mind type
- **Attachment Style** -- How you relate in close relationships

---

## How to Use This Directory

1. **Create a file for each framework you use** (e.g., `strengths-finder.md`, `enneagram.md`)
2. **Include your results and your interpretation** -- not just the type, but what it means for your life
3. **Connect insights to your Areas and Goals** -- "As a Type 5, I need regular solitude to recharge, which means my Health area strategy should include protected alone time"
4. **Reference during quarterly reviews** -- revisit your self-knowledge to check if your current plans honor your nature

---

## A Note on Self-Knowledge

Self-knowledge tools are maps, not territories. No framework captures the full complexity of who you are. Use them as lenses that illuminate different aspects of your nature, not as boxes that constrain you. The goal is greater self-awareness, not a fixed identity.

Hold all frameworks lightly. Use what serves you. Discard what does not.
